The Story of QualityControl

It all started in 2019, with nothing but a pair of clippers, a dream, and an unwavering commitment to perfection. Zaykie R.P didn't just want to cut hair — he wanted to craft confidence. What began as practicing fades on friends in a small garage quickly evolved into something much bigger.

Every cut was a masterpiece in the making. Word spread fast — Zaykie's attention to detail, his ability to envision the perfect style for each client, and his dedication to making every person who sat in his chair feel like royalty. The garage became a destination, and the destination became a brand.

Today, QualityControl isn't just a name — it's a promise. Whether in his personal space or making house calls across the city, Zaykie brings the same level of dedication and artistry to every single cut. From classic fades to modern designs, from clean shaves to complete transformations, the mission remains the same: leave every client looking and feeling their absolute best.
